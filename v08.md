# V08 — Harsh Environments

**Builds on:** v07  |  **Leads to:** v09

## New Features / Engineering Changes
- Conformal-coated PCB option; IP65 enclosure; M12 connector variant
- ATEX/IECEx study + intrinsically-safe barrier integration guide for certified integrators
- Wide-temp parts qualification (−40 °C cold start; 85 °C soak)
- Vibration-rated connectors/cables (locking, strain relief)

## Engineering Disciplines Most Involved
materials, mechanical, reliability, compliance

## Tests Added
- Dedicated validation per features above; regression suite must remain green; acceptance: Survives 1000 h damp-heat (85 °C/85% RH) and 5–500 Hz sweep at 2 g on enclosure.

## Exit Criteria
- Survives 1000 h damp-heat (85 °C/85% RH) and 5–500 Hz sweep at 2 g on enclosure.
